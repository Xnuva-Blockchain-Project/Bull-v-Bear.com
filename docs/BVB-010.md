# BVB-010 — Treasury policy foundation

Pure offline per-currency snapshot and payout quotation module. Mature means gross verified spendable funds before queued obligations, fee budget and protected reserve deductions. Immature and orphaned amounts are informational and must not be included in mature funds. Quotes default to zero when health is unverified or currency disabled. Minimum, maximum and divisor are synthetic policy inputs, not approved live settings. No automatic reward discovery, durable reward ledger, claim integration or atomic quote reservation yet; existing faucet simulation remains unchanged. Seven new tests authored; execution pending. No live payments or production changes. Next: verify regressions and integrate transactional reward accounting and claim reservations before enabling any worker.
