# Supply and market-value adjustment — proposed BVB-010D policy

The faucet must protect mining incentives. Replace the uniform daily ceiling proposal with a supply-adjusted ceiling based on verified original issuance targets, subject to final approval of currency-specific limits. Reserve space for optional market-value adjustment; initial market weighting W=0, price unknown (null), factor M=1. Do not treat unknown price as a real zero price.

Proposed daily ceiling D = min(S * B * M, T * R), where S is relative original issuance supply, B is reference daily allowance, T is verified available treasury, R is approved treasury distribution fraction, and M = (1-W) + W*F. F is a bounded market-price adjustment factor whose exact derivation and bounds remain unapproved. All monetary calculations must use integer units or exact rational arithmetic with conservative rounding. Pending reservations and actual payouts count against rolling limits; no calendar reset or carryover. Re-evaluate safety at payment. Fee and reserve protection remain mandatory.

Market adjustment must remain disabled until separately approved and qualified. Future price inputs require trustworthy sources, liquidity checks, freshness, outlier and manipulation protection; never increase allowances automatically from missing, stale or unreliable quotes. Default to neutral or safer reduced limits on invalid inputs, subject to approved policy. Market adjustment must never override an independently approved hard distribution ceiling. No automatic activation from an exchange listing. No runtime implementation, live payments, mining changes or production deployment authorised by this document. The earlier uniform one-coin ceiling remains a historical proposal; final currency-specific caps must be approved before implementation is qualified.
