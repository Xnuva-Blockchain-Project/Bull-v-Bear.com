How the coins will be distributed

Once claims open, eligible users will receive small amounts of cryptocurrency directly into their own wallets. No purchase or deposit is required.

01 / MINE

Mining-funded

A dedicated share of the project's mining rotation funds the faucet treasury. Visitors' devices will not be used for mining.

02 / CLAIM

Your wallet, your coins

Once launched, eligible visitors will be able to submit a public receiving address for a supported currency. Payouts will depend on matured rewards, available reserves and abuse-prevention limits.

03 / VERIFY

Open and transparent

We aim to provide public payout records and blockchain verification, encouraging users to run their own wallets and independent network nodes.
